Our Culinary Journey

Punjabi Flavours,

At Punjabi Tadka Indian Kitchen, we bring the authentic flavors of Punjabi cuisine to Niagara Falls. With over 30 years of experience, our culinary journey began in Punjab, where our chef’s passion for traditional dishes was born.

We proudly serve as a top Indian restaurant in Niagara Falls, offering the rich, aromatic flavors that make us a favourite for anyone searching for great Punjabi food.

We’ve recently moved to our new home at 5677 Victoria Ave — same recipes, same warmth, brand new location.
